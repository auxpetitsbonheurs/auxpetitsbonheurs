# P2 Owner (Sabera)

Source: PRD section 2; briefs.

## Who
The bakery owner. Runs Aux petits bonheurs, a home bakery, and controls prices, availability, settings and team. At least one active Owner must always exist.

## Context
- Device: her own phone; manages everything from it, one-handed. Installed admin app (PWA) with push.
- Language: EN/FR bakery; admin screens used on a phone.
- Orders arrive today by Instagram DMs, calls and walk-ins.

## Goals
- All orders from any channel in one list, calendar and prep list.
- Approve or decline every website order; none confirmed without her.
- Block any date and set notice per product (no daily cap).
- Change products, prices, photos, promotions and settings without a developer.
- Delegate safely to helpers, with an activity log.
- Zero running cost beyond the domain.

## Frustrations
- Lost or misread orders (dates, prices, options, allergens, messages).
- No single view of what to bake each day.
- Cannot share the work with helpers.
- Fear that a slow approval makes customers abandon (R2); iPhone push needs the installed app (R3).

## Journeys owned (PRD section 7)
J4 Approve and fulfil, J5 Quote step (admin side, shared), J7 Promotion lifecycle, J8 Availability, J9 Admin onboarding and roles, J11 Evening reminder and prep.
