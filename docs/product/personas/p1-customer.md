# P1 Customer (guest or account)

Source: PRD section 2; briefs. Alias: "the Instagram customer".

## Who
Someone in Mauritius who wants a cake, cupcake box, dessert or seasonal special for a specific date. Often arrives from an Instagram link. May be a guest or have an optional account.

## Context
- Device: phone (design baseline 375 px), mid-range, 4G.
- Language: English or French; first visit follows the phone language (FR if French, else EN).
- Pays outside the app (Juice or cash on delivery or pickup).

## Goals
- See what is available and the earliest possible date.
- Get an honest, live price with options, allergens and cake message captured correctly.
- Order in a couple of minutes without chatting back and forth.
- Know the order status (private tracking link, emails in own language).
- Repeat orders quickly (saved details, Reorder).

## Frustrations (today, via DMs)
- Back-and-forth to agree date, price and options.
- Details retyped and misread (dates, allergens, messages).
- Not knowing whether the order is confirmed.

## Journeys owned (PRD section 7)
J1 Guest delivery order, J2 Pickup order, J3 Cancel while pending, J5 Custom cake request (customer side), J10 Returning customer, J12 Bilingual.
