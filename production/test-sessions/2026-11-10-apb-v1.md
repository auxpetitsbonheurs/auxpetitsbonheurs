# Test Session: prototypes/apb-v1 v1 (2026-11-10)

Target: prototypes/apb-v1 v1, shared via ngrok from the developer's laptop: `https://deodorize-designed-turbofan.ngrok-free.dev` (started with `npm run share` in prototypes/apb-v1, then `ngrok http 4173`). Fallback: `npm run dev`, localhost:5173.
Stage: design
Facilitator: [user] (QA Lead structure; ux-researcher observes and takes notes)
Testers: Sabera (bakery owner, client, role Owner), Sarah (admin helper, role Admin), [alias, e.g. "Priya (phone-first cake buyer)"] customer side: no personal data, no real customer details entered

## Goals
1. Can a first-time customer on a phone order a cake (delivery and pickup, EN and FR), cancel, and request a custom cake, without help?
2. Can Sarah and Sabera run an order day (approve, close a day, menu, promotion, team) and does the Owner/Admin split feel right?
3. What are the owner's answers to the open business questions (table below), captured at the matching screen?

## Logistics
- Before the session: the facilitator's laptop must stay awake and online for the whole session (disable sleep). Start `npm run share`, copy the ngrok link, open it once yourself.
- First visit shows an ngrok warning page: tell the client to click **Visit Site**. Then the prototype loads.
- Devices: real phone (375 px) for customer tasks; laptop for admin tasks. Second browser (Firefox or Edge) for a spot check by the facilitator.
- Normal tab (not private) so localStorage persists. Data is per browser: use toolbar Reset data before each tester and after any broken state, and note it in the log. Deep links `/admin` and `/order/<token>` must load directly.
- Facilitator sets the role via the toolbar (Customer / Admin Sarah / Owner Sabera) and keeps a second window on the Outbox to narrate emails and push toasts.
- Timing 60 min: intro 2, Customer 20, Admin 15, Owner 12, post-session 11. If short, drop T6 first. T1 and T5 are must-do.
- Seeded links: `/order/pending-demo-7k2m9xq4` (T3), `/order/approved-demo-9c2v7zk3` (T3), `/order/quote-demo-4h8d1qw2`, `/order/quotesent-demo-3n6b5wy1` (T4).

## Read aloud (about 1 minute)
"Thank you for making time. This is an early draft of the bakery's ordering website and your admin app. It is not the real thing yet, and that is the point: we want your reaction before anything is built.

Everything in it is made up: the customers, orders, prices, the delivery fee, the address and the Juice number are placeholders. Nothing is saved anywhere real, no one will receive an email, and you cannot break anything. If it looks odd or goes wrong, that is the draft's fault, not yours. The page may first show a warning from the sharing tool; click Visit Site.

I will give you a few small situations, like ordering a cake or approving an order. Try them as you would in real life and think out loud: what you are looking at, what you expect, what confuses you. There are no wrong answers and honest criticism helps most. I will mostly stay quiet; if you get stuck I may not answer straight away, because seeing where people get stuck is useful. You can stop at any time. Any questions?"

Probes after each task (neutral): "What were you expecting to happen?" / "What was going through your mind there?" Never "Was that easy?" or "Did you see the button?". Do not hint.

## Exploratory charter (time-box: 15 min, facilitator + customer-side tester)
- Break checkout: back button, refresh mid-flow, double-click Send, quantity 0, negative or under minimum (6 cups), huge or emoji message, empty basket
- Quote expiry: on `/order/quotesent-demo-3n6b5wy1`, use Clock +1 day three times; the quote should close itself and the tracking link must not accept (FR-031, 032)
- If time: phone-in manual order then the prep list for that day (J6, J11 / FR-055..060); promo end around Clock +1 day
- Break roles: Admin by URL (`/admin/more/team`, settings), switch role mid-flow, open another role's tracking link
- Break layout: 320 px, landscape, long French strings on buttons, zoom 200%, large or non-image file in the custom form
- Break state: Reset with an open tracking link; cancel after approval via a stale tab

## Scope notes

- Customer accounts, Reorder, My orders (J10); Gallery management; Sales summary; admin photo upload; drag-reorder (arrows used)
- Placeholder Juice number, address, Rs 200 fee; SVG illustrations instead of photos
- Not testable: invite/deactivate sign-out (rest of J9), 18:00 reminder (J11), backup (NFR-005), performance
