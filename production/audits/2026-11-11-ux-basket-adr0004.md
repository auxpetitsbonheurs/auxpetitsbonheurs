# UX review: client-side basket (ADR-0004)

Date: 2026-11-11 · Reviewer: ux-lead · Scope: basket/checkout page, basket line, product Add, confirmation page.
Inputs: ADR-0004, checkout-and-tracking spec (AC31 amended, AC38, AC39), design system, code in `src/app`, 3 staging screenshots at 375 px.
Method: heuristic review (Nielsen + WCAG 2.2 AA) from code and screenshots. Not run: screen reader, real device, keyboard pass on staging (items marked "verify" need one).

## Verdict

Conditional pass. Instant add, the price-update note, the price-changed refusal flow and touch targets are good and follow the Owner's rule. One decision goes beyond the brief and is not safe as built: placing an order silently without sold-out lines (High 1). It needs a "left out" message before and after the order. Fix the 2 High items before this goes to customers.

## What works (keep)

- Instant feedback: "Added to your basket" lives in an always-mounted `role="status"` (product.tsx:200), the header badge follows the store, and a second status region announces the count (basket-link.tsx:44).
- Price change is calm and in a polite live region per line (basket-line-item.tsx:133). No modal, and checkout stays enabled.
- Price changed between view and submit: the server refuses before anything is committed (place-order.server.ts:93). The page then shows "Total updated from X to Y" (basket.tsx:298), the button reads "Place order at Rs Y" (basket.tsx:319), focus stays on the button and typed fields are kept. The customer therefore always sees the final total before committing. See Medium 4 for the missing "not sent yet".
- Touch targets: stepper `size-11` (button.tsx:28), Remove `min-h-12`, language switch `min-h-11 min-w-11` (language-switch.tsx:25), basket link `size-11` (basket-link.tsx:27), View basket `min-h-11`. All at least 44 px, and 375 px layout does not overflow (screenshots).
- Focus after Remove goes to the "1. Basket" heading (basket-line-item.tsx:50) and the heading is focusable (basket.tsx:239).
- Controls are named per product ("Decrease quantity of {name}"), in both EN and FR.

## High

### H1. Lines left out of the order are never named after the order, and only weakly before

The customer is told only "No longer available" on the dimmed line (basket-line-item.tsx:132). Nothing says "this will not be in your order".

Before the order:
- The checkout summary lists only orderable items (basket.tsx:320, summary-section.tsx:94). The dropped item just disappears from it, with no "Left out" row.
- The dimmed line is in section 1; the Place order bar is several screens below on a phone. A customer who skips past it will not link the two.
- The header badge counts the unavailable line too (basket-link count comes from all lines; screenshot 3 shows badge 2 but one orderable item and a Rs 850 subtotal). The mismatch looks like a bug.

At the moment of submit (stale page):
- If the item sold out after the page loaded, the server drops it and the total no longer matches, so `PRICE_CHANGED` comes back (place-order.server.ts:93).
- `menuWithServerPrices` then flags the line (menu.ts:116-120), so it turns dimmed.
- The only message is "Total updated from Rs X to Rs Y" (basket.tsx:302). The customer is at the bottom of the page, at the Place order button, and the dimmed line is off-screen. They are told the price moved but not why. A customer who presses "Place order at Rs Y" has not been told an item is gone.

After the order:
- The confirmation page shows no items, no total and no left-out note (order-confirmed.tsx:70-103; the loader returns only number and tracking URL). The customer who ignored the dimmed line gets "Thank you, we have your order" for a basket that is missing a cake.
- Check the "order received" email lists items and flags omissions (not reviewed here; if it lists items, the problem is smaller, but the page still must say it).

Fix (all in line with "calm, never blocking", no new modal):
1. In the summary box, add a row per left-out line after the item rows: "{name}: not included, no longer available" (muted, with an icon, not colour alone). Put the same one-sentence note on the dimmed line: "No longer available. It will not be part of your order."
2. When the refusal's lines contain a `sold_out`, `hidden` or `out_of_season` problem, replace the generic total note with one that names the item: "{name} is no longer available and was left out. Total updated from Rs X to Rs Y." Put it in the existing `role="status"` at basket.tsx:343 and add a "Not sent yet" sentence (see M4).
3. Pass the left-out names to the confirmation page without a database change: set a short-lived, non-HttpOnly cookie or a query flag from `submitOrder` (basket.tsx:150) listing the left-out product ids, and render a `Alert tone="warning"`: "Not included in your order: {names}. They were no longer available." Better, and robust to refresh: add the items and totals to the confirmation page (spec FR-025 reads the order from the database anyway), and show "Left out" only from the flag. Decision needed on which; the first is a smaller change, the second is more useful on a refresh.
4. Count the badge from orderable lines, or show unavailable ones as "(1 unavailable)" in the basket heading. Simplest: keep the badge honest by counting only what will be ordered.
5. Add spec AC for the confirmation note and update AC31 in the same change (specs rule: behaviour change updates the spec).

### H2. All lines unavailable shows "Your basket is empty" next to a list of lines

When nothing is orderable, the page still renders the lines section with subtotal and then the empty-basket message (basket.tsx:360-362). Screen: a dimmed "No longer available" line, "Subtotal Rs 0", and "Your basket is empty." That contradicts itself and offers no way forward other than Remove. The server-side refusal gives the same wording (`checkout.errors.basket`, en.json:80: "Your basket is empty.") while lines are still in the basket.

Fix: when lines exist but none are orderable, show a dedicated message instead of the empty text: "Nothing in your basket can be ordered right now. Remove the unavailable items or choose something else." (FR: "Rien dans votre panier ne peut être commandé pour le moment. Retirez les articles indisponibles ou choisissez autre chose.") Keep "See the menu" as the next action, and hide the Rs 0 subtotal. Use the same message for the `VALIDATION_FAILED` `basket` error in the error summary. Reserve "Your basket is empty." for zero lines.

## Medium

### M1. Header badge and basket disagree (see H1.4)
Counts unavailable lines. Screenshot 3: badge 2, one orderable item. Count orderable lines or label the rest.

### M2. A line turning unavailable (or a price rising) is silent for screen readers
The price note is live (basket-line-item.tsx:133), but "No longer available" is plain text rendered at line 132 outside any live region, so nothing is announced when a background menu refresh flips a line. Fix: render the unavailable note inside the same `aria-live="polite"` paragraph (always mounted) so it is announced as "{name}: no longer available. It will not be part of your order." Also say, once per refresh, in a page-level polite region: "1 item is no longer available" (EN) / "1 article n'est plus disponible" (FR).

### M3. Remove has no undo and no announcement
Remove acts instantly with no way back (basket-line-item.tsx:101-104); a line with a cake message or a non-default option is lost. Focus jumps to the heading and a screen reader hears only "1. Basket", not what was removed. Fix: after Remove show a polite status "{name} removed. Undo" with an Undo button (about 6 s, 44 px), restoring the line from the store. At least announce "{name} removed from your basket" in a status region.

### M4. Price-changed refusal does not say the order was not sent
"Total updated from Rs X to Rs Y" (basket.tsx:302) is shown after the customer pressed Place order. They may think it went through or that it failed. The button changes to "Place order at Rs Y" but the status paragraph is a small `text-sm` line (basket.tsx:343) above the bar. Fix: use an `Alert tone="info"` and add the sentence: "Your order has not been sent yet. Check the new total, then press Place order at Rs Y." Make sure it is visible next to the sticky bar after the refusal (scroll it into view without moving focus off the button).
EN: "Your order has not been sent yet. The total changed from {old} to {new}. Press Place order to confirm."
FR: "Votre commande n'a pas encore été envoyée. Le total est passé de {old} à {new}. Appuyez sur Commander pour confirmer."

### M5. Permanent "1 is the smallest quantity" hint on every one-item line
Every default line (quantity 1) shows "1 is the smallest quantity. To take this out, press Remove." (screenshots 2 and 3; basket-line-item.tsx:85-89). It is noise on the normal case and pushes Remove and the totals down. The hint id (`hintId`, line 64) is also not referenced by `aria-describedby`, so a screen reader never ties it to the disabled button. Fix: show it only after the customer taps the disabled minus (or drop it, since Remove is next to the stepper), and link it with `aria-describedby` if kept. Note: a disabled `button` is not focusable, so keyboard users never reach it; consider `aria-disabled` so the hint can be read on focus.

### M6. Repeat taps on Add are swallowed with no feedback
A second tap within 800 ms adds nothing and shows nothing (product.tsx:170); a deliberate second tap after 800 ms shows the identical "Added to your basket" with no sign that it is now 2 (the alert is remounted by `key={added}`, which may or may not re-announce). Fix: show "Added to your basket (2 in basket)" using the line quantity, so the customer can tell the second tap counted. Keep the 800 ms guard.

### M7. FR copy is inconsistent or unnatural
- `basket.unavailableItem`: "Article plus disponible" (fr.json:142) is missing "n'est"; next to "N'est plus disponible" (fr.json:146) it reads as two voices. Use "Cet article n'est plus disponible".
- `basket.noLongerAvailable`: "N'est plus disponible" starts with a bare verb with no subject; use "Plus disponible" or "Cet article n'est plus disponible" (the sentence with the name above).
- `basket.priceUpdated`: "Prix mis à jour de Rs 700 à Rs 850" is a literal translation. Use "Le prix est passé de Rs 700 à Rs 850".
- Native FR review needed for all new strings (i18n rule: copy flagged for human review).

## Low

- L1. Price-up note has the same weight as body text (screenshot 2). Add a small info icon and `font-semibold` for increases so it is not missed; keep it non-alarming. Do not rely on colour.
- L2. The dimmed line with an unknown name shows "Item no longer available" (basket.unavailableItem) and no photo (screenshot 3). Name the product where it is known (hidden products are looked up server-side, but the client loader sets `goneNames: {}` at basket.tsx:79), so a cached basket opens with an anonymous line until the next server render. Keep the last known name in the cache (menu-cache already keeps `goneNames`; verify it survives a cold start with a cookie-only basket).
- L3. Heading order on the page: `h1` "Your basket" then `h2` "1. Basket" is correct, but "Your basket" and "1. Basket" repeat each other. Consider dropping the "1." numbering on small screens or renaming the section "Your items".
- L4. `checkout.errors.form` "Please reload the page and try again." (en.json:88): offer a Reload button rather than only text.
- L5. Verify with a screen reader: the `<output aria-live="polite">` on the stepper (basket-line-item.tsx:77) announces only the number, not the product; each tap announces "2". Prefer a status that says "{name}: quantity 2".
- L6. Verify on a real phone width and keyboard: focus ring visibility on the ghost Remove button and the sticky bar covering a focused field (screenshot 3 shows the sticky bar overlapping the "How would you like to get your order?" heading; `scroll-padding-bottom` for the bar height should be set so focused fields are not hidden, WCAG 2.2 Focus Not Obscured).

## Focus order (as built)

Header: language, logo, basket. Main: `h1` (not focusable), "1. Basket" heading (programmatic target only), then per line stepper (minus, plus) then Remove, then fulfilment, date, time, details, summary, policies, Place order. Order matches the visual order. After Remove, focus goes to the heading, which is correct. After a `PRICE_CHANGED`, focus stays on the button, which is by design but means keyboard and screen-reader users hear nothing unless the status region is read (see M4 and H1.2).

## Suggested order of work

1. H1 (summary row, dimmed-line sentence, named refusal note, confirmation note, badge count) and H2.
2. M2, M4 (announcements and "not sent yet").
3. M3 (undo), M5, M6, M7, then Low.

Out of scope here: visual styling of the new notes (ui-designer), final FR wording (copywriter + native review), accessibility pass with a screen reader (accessibility-specialist).
